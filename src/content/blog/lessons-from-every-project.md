Delivery quality improves when teams treat mistakes as data rather than failure. I document blockers, estimate misses and communication gaps after each project.

Patterns appear quickly: weak discovery causes scope churn, unclear ownership causes delays and missing QA gates cause avoidable regressions.

Retrospectives are not bureaucracy. They are one of the fastest ways to raise project quality without increasing budget.
