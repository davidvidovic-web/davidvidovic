Case-study breakdowns matter because they turn subjective output into repeatable process. I document what changed, why it changed and how users responded after release.

For every launch, I track a small set of directional metrics: conversion behavior, session depth and friction points in forms or checkout flows. This keeps post-launch decisions grounded in real usage.

The strongest portfolio pieces are transparent about trade-offs. Showing constraints and decisions builds more trust than polished screenshots alone.
