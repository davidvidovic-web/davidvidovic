Design decisions are rarely linear. Some days start in Figma, others in code, and sometimes the most valuable progress is stepping back to simplify the problem.

I keep early drafts intentionally rough. It is faster to reject a weak direction at low fidelity than to polish the wrong concept.

When feedback loops stay short, project quality increases without adding noise. The goal is clear execution, not creative chaos.
