## The idea

Businesses answer many of the same questions across their websites and stores, but useful answers depend on information scattered across pages, documents and internal guidance. Pitaj.io brings that knowledge into a configurable AI agent for customer support and sales, with a path from an initial question to a useful next step.

I built Pitaj as a product made up of a dashboard, API, embeddable chat widget and integrations. The goal was to make a complex workflow approachable: give a business a way to configure an agent around its own information, test it and put it in front of customers.

## From knowledge to conversation

The agent setup starts with a name, language and role. Businesses can add knowledge from a website, documents, text snippets and question-and-answer pairs. That content provides context for customer conversations, while a live preview makes it possible to test the experience as the agent is configured.

The widget designer brings those conversations into the business's visual identity, with controls for appearance, greetings and suggested questions. The resulting widget can be embedded on a website, with WordPress and Shopify integrations providing additional deployment paths.

## Actions and workflows

An answer is often only the beginning of a customer journey. Pitaj's documented actions include lead capture, links and buttons, external API calls and Shopify commerce interactions. Procedures add structured steps and branching for situations that need a defined process, including support escalation and after-hours handling.

The product connects these pieces through a Next.js and React dashboard, a Laravel API and a separate chat widget. WordPress and Shopify projects extend the experience into existing websites and stores.

## The outcome

Pitaj brings agent configuration, business knowledge and the customer-facing widget into a single product experience. It demonstrates how I approach product development across the interface, application architecture and integrations, with the aim of making AI useful within everyday business workflows.
