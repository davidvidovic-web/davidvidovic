# Objective

A beautiful and functional website for a professional beauty salon, featuring online booking, service showcase, and client testimonials to enhance their digital presence and streamline appointment management.

# Process

Developed a custom WordPress theme with integrated booking functionality, implemented a responsive design for mobile users, and created an intuitive service catalog with detailed descriptions and pricing information.

# Results

The salon saw a 50% increase in online bookings within the first month, reduced phone call volume by 40%, and improved customer satisfaction with the convenient online booking experience.

# Counters

- 50% | Increase in online bookings
- 40% | Reduction in phone calls
- 90% | Customer satisfaction rate
